# Privacy Policy — College Buddy: Attendance

**Last updated: 20 April 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how College Buddy ("the App", "we", "us") handles your information.

## TL;DR

College Buddy is privacy-first by design. Your attendance data, subjects, timetable, assignments, and notes are stored **on your device only**. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. We literally cannot see your data because it never leaves your iPhone.

The only exception is your subscription status, which is handled directly by Apple through the App Store.

---

## 1. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store on any server:

- Your name, email address, phone number, or any other identifier
- Your attendance records, subjects, timetable, assignments, or notes
- Your usage of the app (no analytics)
- Your location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data
- Any device identifiers (IDFA, advertising ID)
- Crash reports, performance data, or diagnostic information

The App does not contain any third-party SDKs that collect data — no Google Analytics, no Facebook SDK, no Firebase, no Mixpanel, no AppsFlyer, no Adjust, nothing.

---

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

The following information is stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's standard storage APIs (`UserDefaults` and the App Group container). It never leaves your device:

- **Subjects** — names, attendance counts, attendance thresholds, colors
- **Timetable** — your weekly class schedule
- **Assignments** — titles, due dates, status, notes
- **Attendance history** — a log of present/absent entries
- **Settings** — notification time, subscription status cache

You can delete all of this at any time using **Settings → Reset Semester** inside the app, or by uninstalling the app.

---

## 3. Subscriptions and In-App Purchases

College Buddy offers optional Pro subscriptions and a one-time Lifetime unlock through Apple's StoreKit framework. When you make a purchase:

- **Apple** processes the transaction. You enter your Apple ID and payment details into Apple's interface, not ours.
- **We receive only**: a verified receipt from Apple confirming whether you have an active subscription or completed purchase. This receipt does not contain your name, email, payment details, or Apple ID.
- The receipt is used solely to unlock Pro features inside the app.

To manage or cancel your subscription, go to **iPhone Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → College Buddy**.

For refunds, visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

Apple's privacy policy: [apple.com/legal/privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

---

## 4. Notifications

If you enable daily attendance reminders, the App schedules **local notifications** on your device using Apple's `UserNotifications` framework. These notifications are generated and delivered entirely by your iPhone — no server is involved.

You can disable notifications at any time via the iPhone's **Settings → Notifications → College Buddy**, or inside the app under **Settings**.

---

## 5. Widgets

The App provides home-screen widgets that read your subjects and timetable from the App Group container on your device. The widget code runs locally on your iPhone and does not transmit data anywhere.

---

## 6. iCloud

The current version of College Buddy stores data only on the device it is installed on. Data does **not** automatically sync to other devices via iCloud. If iCloud sync is added in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated and you will be notified inside the app.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

College Buddy is suitable for users aged 4 and above. We do not knowingly collect any information from children — because we do not collect information from anyone.

---

## 8. Data Security

Because all data lives on your device, its security is governed by Apple's iOS security model — including device passcode, Face ID / Touch ID, and full-disk encryption. We recommend keeping your iPhone updated to the latest version of iOS.

---

## 9. Your Rights

Since we do not collect or hold any personal data:

- There is nothing for you to **access** (you already have it on your device)
- There is nothing for you to **rectify** (you can edit it directly in the app)
- To **erase** all data, use **Settings → Reset Semester** in the app, or uninstall the app

If you live in the EU, UK, California, or any other jurisdiction with data protection laws — the same rights apply, but there is simply no data on our side to act upon.

---

## 10. Third-Party Services

College Buddy does not integrate any third-party services other than Apple's own (StoreKit for purchases, UserNotifications for reminders, WidgetKit for widgets). No data is shared with any third party.

---

## 11. Changes to This Policy

If we change how the App handles data — for example, if we add iCloud sync — we will update this Privacy Policy and update the "Last updated" date at the top. For material changes, we will display a notice inside the app.
