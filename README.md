## Common Questions

### How do I cancel my subscription?

- **iPhone** — Settings → tap your name at the top → Subscriptions → College Buddy → Cancel
- **Android** — Google Play → tap your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → College Buddy → Cancel

Cancelling stops the next renewal. You keep Pro until the end of the period you have already paid for.

### My attendance % isn't updating
Make sure the class name in your timetable matches your subject name exactly (case and spaces don't matter, but spelling does).

### Can I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by the store, not by us.

- **Apple** — [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com)
- **Google** — [support.google.com/googleplay](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637)

### Will my data sync to other devices?

- **iPhone** — yes, through your own iCloud account.
- **Android** — not yet. Data is stored on the one device, so uninstalling the app or resetting the semester deletes it.

### I'm not getting class reminders
Check that notifications are allowed for College Buddy, and that battery optimisation isn't restricting it. On Android 12 and later, exact reminders also need the alarm permission — there's a shortcut to it in **Settings → Notifications → Exact alarm permission**.

### How do I reset for a new semester?
Settings → Reset Semester. This clears all subjects, attendance, timetable, assignments and planner tasks. It cannot be undone.
