## Common Questions

### How do I cancel my subscription?
iPhone Settings → tap your name at the top → Subscriptions → College Buddy → Cancel.

### My attendance % isn't updating
Make sure the class name in your timetable matches your subject name exactly (case and spaces don't matter, but spelling does).

### Can I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) and select your College Buddy purchase.

### Will my data sync to other devices?
Currently, College Buddy stores data locally on your device. iCloud sync is coming in a future update.

### How do I reset for a new semester?
Settings → Reset Semester. This clears all subjects, attendance, timetable, and assignments.
